not confirmed 904-0002-25 is what Landmark Machinery lists as Bandit's number for this switch. Have the counter confirm it against the serial above. 25-05-0109 looks like the case number, not the order number.

Ask the counter

  1. "Look up the Murphy shutdown switch for this serial number." Confirm it's 904-0002-25.
  2. Which Murphy model is it: 518PH-12, 518APH-12 or 518E? Murphy recommends the 518E for chippers because it holds up to vibration better.
  3. If it's in stock, compare the terminals on the new one with ours before leaving: 3 terminals = 117, 5–6 terminals = 518 series.
  4. Price, and whether they also carry the oil-pressure and water-temperature senders for this engine.

Before buying — check this first

Owners on forums say these switches often get blamed for shutdowns that are really caused by a bad oil-pressure or water-temperature sender. The switch only reacts to those. If the chipper keeps shutting down, test the senders before replacing the switch.
