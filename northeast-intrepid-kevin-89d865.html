Kevin — here's the opportunity I want you in on, and exactly what's in it for you.
From Doug Brown · Second Nature Tree · June 2026

I'm setting up Northeast Intrepid — the Northeast dealer and demo center for Intrepid compact loaders (the Belgian Knikmops line, imported by Intrepid Loaders USA). They sell ~20–30 machines a year nationally with no real presence up here, no demos, no local service. I'm building that presence — and I want you and your trucks as the transport & service side of it.

Why I'm asking you

Every machine that sells has to get delivered, set up, and kept running. That's you. You've got the trucks, the equipment know-how, and you're someone I trust to do it right. I don't want to build a dealership and then scramble on service — I want it locked before I even go back to the importer.

What the role is

🚚

Transport & delivery

Haul and deliver machines to buyers and demos across the Hudson Valley corridor.

🔧

Setup & service

Pre-delivery setup and inspection, plus repairs on the heavier iron.

🛡️

Warranty work

Handle warranty repairs locally instead of machines shipping back across the country.

📦

Parts & maintenance

Parts installs and routine maintenance that keep customers coming back.

What's in it for you

  • A recurring revenue stream — every machine sold means delivery, setup, and ongoing service work flowing to you, not a one-off.
  • You're the first call on every Intrepid in the region — as the dealership grows, so does your service book. You become the Intrepid service name up here.
  • It fits what you already do — heavy hauling and equipment work. This adds a steady lane on top of your existing business, it doesn't replace it.
  • You keep your own business — this is a partnership, not a job. You run Kevin's Heavy Trucking; you invoice for the work.
  • Ground floor — get in as we start and grow with it (KM85 up through the KM250, plus the Jobeau chippers and stump grinders — a whole green-industry line).

How it would work

  • By appointment / as scheduled — we set fair response targets so customers aren't left waiting.
  • You invoice the work — pay terms we agree on up front numbers to set together.
  • Parts come through the dealer/importer pipeline; warranty jobs get documented so they're paid.
  • Independent contractor — you stay your own boss; we'll confirm the tax/insurance side properly.
  • It's all written up in a simple Service Partner Agreement (already drafted) — nothing locked until you've read it and we've talked terms.

Where it's at

This is early but real. The plan: I buy my own first unit, lock service (you + Ron), then go to the importer for consignment inventory to demo and sell. Having you on board before that conversation is what makes the whole thing credible.

Pay rates and volume aren't set yet — I'm not going to throw made-up numbers at you. Let's talk through what's fair for delivery, setup, and shop time, and put it in the agreement.

Next step

Take a look, think about it, and let's sit down. I'll walk you through the Service Partner Agreement and we'll work out the terms together. If you're in, you're in at the start — and the service side of every Intrepid in the Northeast runs through your shop.
