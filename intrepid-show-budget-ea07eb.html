The realistic 2026 calendar — what actually makes sense

Driving the machine only makes sense for shows you can reach in a day or two. The far national shows get cut (towing a loader to Florida or Kentucky burns a week and ~$900–$930 in fuel each way — not worth it as a new dealer). Anchor the year on the in-region demo shows:

Cut from the drive list: TCI EXPO (Ft. Lauderdale, Nov) and Equip Expo (Louisville, Oct) — best buyers and demo yards, but 1,200+ mi each way. Fly to TCI without the machine only once you're established. Paul Bunyan (OH, ~445 mi) is the one borderline drive — include it if a fall trip pencils out.
