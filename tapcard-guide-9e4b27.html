📇 Tap to Share — your digital business card

One link that puts your name, number, and email straight into anyone's phone. No paper cards. Here's how to set it up and use it.
Open Doug's card →

1 · Put the card on your home screen (2 minutes)

  1. Open the card link above in Safari on your iPhone.
  2. Tap the Share button (square with the arrow, bottom center).
  3. Scroll down, tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
Now it sits on your phone like an app. Meet someone → tap the icon → hand them the phone → they hit "Save Contact." Done.

2 · The real "tap phones" trick — NFC sticker (10 minutes, ~$10)

  1. Buy NTAG215 NFC stickers (Amazon, ~$10 for 10+).
  2. Get the free "NFC Tools" app from the App Store (Apple's built-in Shortcuts NFC only works for your own phone — NFC Tools writes the link onto the sticker so anyone's phone can read it).
  3. In NFC Tools: Write → Add a record → URL → paste:
    https://branchmanager.app/card-f8cee7a8.html
  4. Tap Write, hold the sticker to the top of your phone until it confirms.
  5. Stick it on your phone case, clipboard, or the truck door.
Anyone who taps their iPhone (top edge) or Android to the sticker gets the card instantly — nothing to install, works even when your phone is dead because the sticker does the work.

3 · Other ways to fire it off

  1. Text it: just send the card link in a text — tapping it opens the card.
  2. From the card itself: the card page has a Share button (uses your phone's normal share sheet) and a "Save Contact" that downloads the .vcf contact file.
