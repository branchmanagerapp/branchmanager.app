At 1 employee: Shopify is $265/yr cheaper — and it still is not worth it

Shopify Basic with free POS Lite comes to $8,861 against Square Free at $9,126. That $265 gap is erased by a single hardware purchase, and it only holds while exactly one person can ring a sale. It is a real number, but it is not a reason to move.

At 7 employees: Square wins by $803/yr

Seven people at the register forces POS Pro at $89/mo — Shopify's plan tiers cap staff seats at 2 (Basic), 5 (Grow) and 15 (Advanced), and only POS Pro lifts the register PIN limit. That pushes Shopify to $9,929 against Square Free at $9,126. Square charges nothing per employee, so its number does not move at all between these two columns.

The Premium plan question

$1,040/yr, sitting on the table

Premium buys you 0.2% off in-person and 0.4% off card-not-present. On your volume that is worth $748/yr. It costs $1,788/yr. You are paying $1,040 for a discount you are too small to use.

Break-even is about $660,000 in annual card volume. You are at $276,185 — 42% of the way there. Even a very good year does not close that gap.

What is not in these numbers

  • Shopify hardware. Square readers are not compatible. New readers required — not verified, depends on station count.
  • Migration labour. 1,102 items, 1,557 variations, 88 categories, plus customer and order history.
  • Square Payroll, if you use it: $35/mo + $6/employee. At 7 staff that is $77/mo — but it is payroll, not POS, and Shopify has no native equivalent. I have not verified whether you use it.
  • Downtime risk at the counter, which you have already said you will not accept.
