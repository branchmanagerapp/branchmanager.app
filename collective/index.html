  1. 1. Lead comes in Request

    Customer texts about a big maple over the garage. It lands as a Request with name, address & notes — nothing slips.

  2. 2. You build the quote Quote

  3. 3. Approved → it schedules Schedule

    Becomes a Job. Drag it onto a day, assign the crew — shows on the shared calendar & map so the whole Collective sees who's got what.

  4. 4. Crew & cost breakdown Job

  5. 5. Invoice → paid Invoice + Payment

    Invoice generates from the same line items, sends with a card-payment link, and the deposit auto-matches your bank feed.
