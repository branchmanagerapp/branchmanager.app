Parts department

Genuine parts, stocked local, shipped fast

The engines are Kubota diesels — proven, and parts are everywhere. We keep the common service and wear items on the shelf and ship them out the same or next day, so a buyer who's down a filter or a fitting isn't waiting weeks on an overseas order.

  • Proven Kubota power — KM85 runs a D902, KM100 a D1105, KM130 a V1505 — all from Kubota's most common small-diesel family, so parts are everywhere and never a special order.
  • On the shelf: oil, fuel, and air filters, belts, glow plugs, hydraulic filters, and the common wear items.
  • Shipped fast — order it, it ships same or next day from our parts bench.
  • Service kits for routine maintenance so you keep the machine, not the downtime.

Financing

Own one without the whole price up front

Most buyers finance, and we make it simple — competitive equipment financing through established lenders, with promotional rates when they're available.

Options we can line up

0% APR up to 36 months (qualifying machines) Terms 24–72 months $0-down for qualified buyers Lease-to-own Start-up & credit-challenged programs Section 179 tax write-off

Standard financing through Sheffield Financial, Geneva Capital, and Stearns Bank (rate set by your credit and the machine), plus 0% factory promos through Intrepid when they're running, on qualifying machines. Lease-to-own is popular with landscapers: low monthly payments, ownership at lease end, and it can stay off your balance sheet. Tell us your budget and we'll find the structure that fits — monthly payment first, machine second.

Financing provided by third-party lenders; rates, terms, and approval subject to lender credit criteria. Ask your accountant about the Section 179 deduction.
