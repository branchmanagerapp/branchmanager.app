Parts department

Genuine parts, stocked local, shipped fast

The engines are Kubota diesels — proven, and parts are everywhere. We keep the common service and wear items on the shelf and ship them out the same or next day from our own bench, so a buyer who's down a filter or a fitting isn't waiting weeks on an overseas order.

  • Proven Kubota power — the KM85 runs a Kubota D902, the KM100 a D1105, the KM130 a V1505 — all from Kubota's most common small-diesel family, so filters and parts are everywhere and never a special order.
  • On the shelf: oil, fuel, and air filters, belts, glow plugs, hydraulic filters, and the common wear items.
  • Shipped fast — order it, it ships same or next day from our parts bench.
  • Genuine parts, matched to your exact engine — no guessing on fitment.
  • Service kits for routine maintenance so you keep the machine, not the downtime.
