🪓 How a job breaks down

Example: large maple removal, crane-assisted — Peekskill

  1. 1. Lead comes in Request

    Customer calls/texts: big maple over the garage. It lands as a Request with their name, address, and notes — nothing falls through the cracks.
    2. ↓

  2. 2. You build the quote Quote

  3. 3. Approved → it schedules itself Schedule

    Approved quote becomes a Job. Drag it onto a day — say Tue 6/16 — assign the crew, and it shows on the calendar + map. Everybody sees the same plan (no more "I thought you had Mike Thursday").
    4. ↓

  4. 4. Crew & cost breakdown Job

    What it costs youCost
    Crew — climber + 2 ground (day)$1,050.00

  5. 5. Invoice → paid Invoice + Payment

  6. 6. Your books update on their own Books

    This one job: Revenue $5,150  −  Cost $2,270  =  $2,880 profit
    Live P&L, sales-tax set aside, every job's margin tracked — no shoebox of receipts at tax time.
