RealWork Labs, Google, and the Recent Work feed

Where it stands on Sat Sept 19, 2026: what is blocking "get found by the neighbors where we actually work," whose move each blocker is, and whether RealWork is worth paying for.

The short versionRealWork Labs sells geotagged job posts that show up on your site and feed Google. You already built the same thing for free: every approved work day becomes a page on peekskilltree.com/recent-work with the town, the photos and the schema Google reads. It is showing zero jobs today because nothing has been approved yet, not because it does not work. Do not pay RealWork until the free feed has 30 days of approved jobs on it and you can see whether the map calls move. Then decide with data.

The three blockers, in order

1 · Nothing approved, so the public feed is empty your tap15 photo days sit as drafts in SocialBranch (Wilton 9/14 plus 14 August days). Approving one publishes it to the Recent Work map and its own page within a minute. Until the first approve, Google has nothing new to index and RealWork looks like the only option. It is not.
2 · Google Business posting is not connected your loginNative Google posting from SocialBranch needs a Google OAuth client and Google's API-access approval. The approval case 1-1528000040893 was filed Apr 23 and never confirmed; the OAuth client secrets are still unset in the new functions. Result: the Wednesday post series cannot reach Google Business. Fastest check: the April Make.com scenario (id 4839929) posted to Facebook, Instagram and Google Business and was verified end to end. If that scenario is still active in your Make account, pasting its webhook back into SocialBranch → Accounts turns posting on today with no Meta or Google paperwork.
3 · Meta developer registration is stuck on an SMS that never arrives your phoneFacebook and Instagram native posting need a Meta app. On Sept 13 Meta's verification text never reached any of your numbers over 90 minutes. Options: try the code by voice call or WhatsApp on the Meta wizard, or skip Meta entirely by using the Make webhook above.

What RealWork Labs actually offers

Geotagged review and job posts, a site widget, Google feedFrom Eli Johnson's Sept 11 email and the demo site lynx.realworklabs.com: check-ins from the field get pinned to the address, reviews get tied to the neighborhood, a widget shows it on your site. Price was not in writing; you recalled roughly $200/month. Their pitch is real and the mechanism works, which is exactly why the free version was built into Branch Manager: the photo ingest already knows the address, the date and the client for every job day.
Where the free version is aheadYour per-day pages carry Service and Place schema, link to the matching town page, and use your own photos. RealWork posts live on their subdomain. Where RealWork is ahead: review capture on the truck, which Branch Manager's satisfaction page (sat.html) does after the job but not on site.
