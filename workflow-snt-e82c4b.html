Field rules

  • Gave a price on-site? Put it in the Jobber quote before you drive away.
  • Finished a job? Invoice it that day. A done job that isn't invoiced is invisible money.
  • Customer said yes by text or phone? Mark the quote approved yourself.
  • Got paid by Zelle, cash or check? Record the payment in Jobber the same day.
