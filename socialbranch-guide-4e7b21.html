SocialBranch in 5 minutes

Catherine, this is the social posting tool inside Branch Manager. Claude drafts posts, you approve them. Nothing goes out until you say so.

Open it

Open Marketing in Branch Manager

Log in with your usual Branch Manager login (cat.conway@gmail.com). On the phone the tabs scroll sideways: Dashboard Compose Calendar Media Accounts. You only need three of them.

Tip: add it to your home screen. In Safari tap the Share button, then "Add to Home Screen". It opens like an app after that.

Approve a post Claude drafted

1
Dashboard tabDrafts are listed there. Anything marked draft is waiting on you. Tap one to open it in Compose.
2
Read the caption, fix anything you wantIt's a normal text box. Change words, add a line, whatever reads like us. The Live Preview under it shows what people will see.
3
Check the photoSwap it with "Pick from Media Center" or "Upload" if you have a better one from your camera roll.
4
Check the networksFacebook, Instagram, Google Business are checkboxes. Leave the ones Claude picked unless you want to add or drop one.
5
Pick the dateThe "Schedule for" box already has a suggested day. Change it if you like. Empty means post right now.
6
Tap Publish / ScheduleThat's the approval. You'll see "Scheduled for …". Not ready? Tap "Save draft" instead and it stays put.

Write your own post

  1. Tap New Post (top right) or the Compose tab.
  2. Type the caption, or tap AI Caption and it writes one from the photo. Edit it so it sounds like you.
  3. Add a photo: Upload from your phone, or Pick from Media Center for job photos already in Branch Manager.
  4. Tick the networks, pick a date, tap Publish / Schedule.

Good weekly rhythm: one post, one real photo, two or three plain sentences. Before/after shots, a crew photo, a tree we saved, a thank-you. No prices, ever.

See what's coming

Calendar tab shows every scheduled post by day. Drag one to another day to move it. Media tab is the photo library. Everything you upload lands there and can be reused.

The one thing that has to be connected

Facebook, Instagram and Google Business connect directly in the Accounts tab (tap Connect, log in on their page once, done). Until a network shows ✓ Connected there, a scheduled post sits in the calendar and does not publish. Doug is finishing that. You can still approve posts now, and they'll go out on their own once the networks are connected.

Rule of the house: Claude drafts, a person approves. Nothing on this page posts anything on its own.
