🎙️ Tracking my words — 24/7

How many words I say in a day, and my share vs everyone else in a conversation. The real options, honestly compared.

Heads up: I searched your memory, the site, and your whole Mac — there wasn't an existing page on this in this account (might be on your other one). So this is a fresh build for you to react to.

What you actually want to measure

  • Your daily word count — total words you speak from wake to sleep.
  • Your share of the conversation — you vs the other person(s). This is the interesting one.

The tech that makes the second one possible is speaker diarization — software that separates "you" from "them" in a recording, so each person gets their own word count. Every option below lives or dies on how well it does that.

The two capture routes

To count words 24/7 you need audio running all day. Two ways: wear a dedicated little recorder, or carry an always-on phone (your "another iPhone" idea).

Option 1 — a wearable AI recorder (least fuss)

Limitless Pendant easiest 24/7

Clip-on pendant made for exactly this — records your day, transcribes, and separates speakers, with an app that shows who talked. All-day battery.

✓ Built for all-day wear + speaker separation out of the box · owns the "who said what" problem

✗ Your data lives on their cloud · monthly plan for full features

Bee (AI wristband) cheapest

A ~wristband that always listens and builds a life-log with transcripts. Cheap entry point.

✓ Inexpensive · truly always-on · wrist-worn (easy to keep on)

✗ Lighter on precise per-speaker word analytics · cloud-based

Plaud (NotePin / Note) solid

Wearable clip / card recorder with strong AI transcription + summaries; popular for meetings.

✓ Great transcription quality · discreet

✗ More meeting-oriented than 24/7 life-logging · AI needs a subscription

Option 2 — a spare iPhone as the recorder (your idea)

Dedicated iPhone + Otter.ai fast to try

Put a recorder app on a spare iPhone, keep it in your pocket charged. Otter transcribes and already reports "talk time" per speaker — closest off-the-shelf thing to your words-vs-theirs metric.

✓ You may already have the phone · high mic quality · Otter shows talk-share

✗ iOS limits true all-day background recording (works best foreground / screen-on) · battery + heat for 24/7 · you'll manage storage

Spare iPhone → your own pipeline most control

Phone records raw audio all day → each night it uploads → transcription with diarization (Whisper or AssemblyAI) → a simple daily dashboard: your word count + your % of the talking. Could even live inside Branch Manager.

✓ You own 100% of the data · exact custom metric (your words vs theirs, per day, per person) · no per-device subscription

✗ It's a build (I'd set it up) · transcription has a per-hour cost · same iOS all-day-recording limits to design around

My honest read

  • Want it working this week, hands-off? Get a Limitless Pendant (or Bee if you want cheap) — it wears all day and separates speakers without you building anything.
  • Want to own the data and get the exact "my words vs theirs" number your way? Use the spare iPhone + let me build the record→diarize→dashboard pipeline. More powerful, and it's yours.
  • Reasonable middle: spare iPhone + Otter now to see if the metric even tells you something useful, then graduate to the custom pipeline if you like it.
⚖️ Quick legal note (not legal advice): New York is a one-party consent state, so you may lawfully record conversations you're part of. Be considerate about others' privacy, especially where people expect it. Confirm current device prices/specs before buying — this is a review as of early 2026, not a live catalog.
