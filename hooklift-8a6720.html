The honest gut-check

The numbers say consolidate. Killing the Ram's $1,004.91/mo payment is the whole story — it turns a $50k purchase into a cash-positive move before you haul a single extra log. The F550 + Ram sales cover most of the truck, so you finance almost nothing.

On the F550 price: $35k is your floor — fair, given the work you've put in, and it's your call what you'll accept. Just know a buyer prices a 2004 off condition + documented upgrades (receipts, new drivetrain, recent rebuild), not effort. If it won't move at $35k, the real outcome is you keep it — which the "keep all 3" column already prices. Either way the consolidation still works; it just funds less of the truck.

The thing the math can't see: one Class-7 hook-lift can't be everywhere. If you regularly need a chipper running while a truck hauls, or a non-CDL driver to run errands/estimates, one truck won't cover it and you'll re-buy a pickup — wiping out the savings. Consolidate only if the crew + remaining vehicles genuinely cover daily/light-duty. The truck sales cover most of the cost — finance the small remainder.
