Photos → pages, automatically

"All I wanna do is take photos and it just be done." Here is exactly what stands between that and today, measured against your real camera roll, and the build that closes it.

The one thing only you can do: turn off iCloud photo optimization on the Mac. Photos app → Settings → iCloud → Download Originals to this Mac. Right now the Mac holds only thumbnails for most of your recent shots (117 of the last 263 GPS photos are 1 KB placeholders). Every automated run will keep producing thumbnails until that switch is flipped. Nothing else on this page needs you.

The gaps, plainly

  1. Photos never reach Branch Manager. Branch Cam exists, nobody uses it. Your photos live in iCloud; Catherine's live on her phone. Fix: read your Mac's Photos library nightly (proven Aug 23), and give Catherine a shared iCloud album to drop into. No new habits.
  2. Most of what is on the Mac is a thumbnail. Fix: the iCloud switch above. Until then the pipeline uses whatever full-size copies exist (26 of 121 right now).
  3. Work days exist without a job record. 17 of 21 client-days had no Branch Manager job that date, so nothing can attach to a job. Fix: stop requiring a job. A photo cluster at a client address on a date is the work-day record. It gets a job number if one exists, otherwise it stands alone.
  4. GPS cannot tell work from the town park. Fix: an approve queue. Drafts land in a private bucket; you tap Approve or Skip on your phone; only approved days go public. One tap per work day, usually 2–4 a week.
  5. Nothing is published. peekskilltree.com has no map and no job pages; the 36 town pages have no proof of work behind them. Fix: a public feed + a "Recent work near you" map + one page per approved work day, street-level, no names, no prices.
  6. Google never hears about it. Fix: the same approved card becomes a draft Google Business Profile post in SocialBranch. You approve, it posts.

The build — five pieces, in order

1. Nightly ingest on the Mac nextReads Photos.sqlite read-only → GPS photos since last run → drop yard + anything not within 150 m of a client → group by client + day → upload full-size copies to a private bucket → one draft work_days row per client-day (town, date, photo list, matched job # if any). Runs at 9 pm on this Mac now, moves to the mini later. Personal photos never leave the Mac.
2. Approve page (private, phone) nextOne unlisted page: each draft shows its photos, town, date, client. Buttons: Approve · Skip · Pick cover. Approve copies the photos to the public bucket and flips the row. Nothing sends, nothing posts.
3. Public feedEdge function returning approved work days only: town, month, service, cover + 2 photos, coordinates rounded to ~150 m. No names, no addresses, no money. Cached, CORS open.
4. peekskilltree.com/work — the map + job pagesLeaflet map of approved work days with a card list, plus /work/<town>-<date>-<id>/ pages with Service + Place schema, linked from the matching town page. Generated nightly by the same job and pushed to the live repo (the PAT in the deploy clone works; verified today).
5. Google Business Profile draftsApproved card → SocialBranch draft post with the cover photo and town. You approve in SocialBranch; it posts. Same rule as always: nothing auto-fires to the public.

What stays true

  • No prices, no customer names, no house numbers on anything public.
  • Drafts live in a private bucket; only approved days are public.
  • You approve; the system never posts on its own.
  • Catherine's photos: shared iCloud album "Job photos" → same pipeline. Branch Cam stays as the optional in-app path.
Effort. Pieces 1–2 are one build session and start paying off the night they run. Pieces 3–4 are a second session. Piece 5 is an hour once 4 exists. The iCloud switch is the only thing gating quality; everything else can be built before you flip it.
