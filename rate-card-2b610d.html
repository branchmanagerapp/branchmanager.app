How it works — two coverage tiers

Uninsured

You bring your own coverage

Lower rate. You provide a Certificate of Insurance (GL + Workers' Comp) naming Second Nature as additional insured before pickup. You own the liability while it's in your hands.

Insured

Covered under Second Nature

Higher rate — the add-on buys coverage under SNT's Erie GL + NYSIF Workers' Comp, so you don't need your own policy. Simplest if you're not fully insured yet.
