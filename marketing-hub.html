02 · The accountability system

Posting happens when it's already done. So we post in advance.

The plans are worthless if nobody posts. The fix isn't willpower — it's batching a month of content ahead of time, so posting doesn't depend on anyone remembering on a Tuesday. Content that exists before the week starts goes out. Content that doesn't, doesn't.

This is safe — it's the opposite of May 6. That was auto-generated email blasting customers with no human gate. This is a human-approved batch that publishes on a schedule. The approval just moves up front. Nothing hits a customer channel that you didn't sign off on in the batch.
You already built the engine — use it. Branch Manager has SocialBranch (socialbranch.js, "replaces SocialPilot"): compose, calendar, draft→scheduled→posted, publishing via Zapier/Make webhook or direct GMB/Meta APIs. It's live for tree. Skate and lawn get their own ported copies, each wired to that brand's own accounts — separate stores so they can't cross.

The monthly ritual (≈90 min, once a month, per brand)

1Pull the month's contentEach brand's engine feeds it: skate = clips, tree = job before/afters + reviews, lawn = install/demo clips + the map tool. Brand-pure.
2Fill the calendar from the plan's rhythmEach marketing plan already defines the weekly cadence. Drop the month's posts into the grid below (Claude can draft the captions).
3Approve the batchOne review pass. This is the gate — after this it's hands-off.
4Load into each brand's Business SuiteSchedule all 30 days into that brand's own IG/FB (+ TikTok). Separate logins = structurally can't cross brands.
