⛈ The Storm Desk — run by Andrew

Storms are the best-paying work in tree care and the worst-run. The crews that get paid are the ones with someone running the desk. That's you.

What Andrew runs (the desk)

  • Lands the work — property managers, Servpro-type nationals, insurance contacts. You already have these relationships.
  • Admin that gets everyone paid — portal-to-portal billing, hours + equipment logs, photo documentation for approvals.
  • Compliance — W-9s, COIs, workers' comp docs, payroll onboarding. Nobody swings a saw until their paperwork's in.
  • Payroll — one desk cuts the checks; crews don't chase money.

What the crews bring

  • Trucks, chippers, buckets, climbers — at each operator's day rates.
  • Fast response when the call comes — the desk's promise to the client only works if crews roll.
  • Hours + photos texted to the desk daily, no exceptions — that's what turns work into approved invoices.
The split (proposed — let's talk it through): the desk takes ~10% of billings for landing the work and running admin/payroll/compliance. Crews keep their labor + equipment rates. Simple, same for everyone, agreed before the first storm — not during one.

Why this works — we already did it

Roscoe, July 2026: tornado cleanup for a national property manager. Andrew landed it, ran portal-to-portal billing, collected everyone's W-9s and COIs, processed ~$53k of payroll, and every crew got paid. That wasn't luck — that was the desk. This just makes it standing instead of one-off.
