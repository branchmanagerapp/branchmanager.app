Placement (from the research)

  • Next to fresh installs — signs near a recent job convert ~4× better than cold spots; neighbors saw the mower running.
  • Intersections with a stop sign or light beat open road — drivers are stationary and actually read.
  • Letters read ~10 ft per inch of height — the big lines on these signs are readable from ~150 ft.
  • QR scans happen from parked/stopped cars and walkers, not moving traffic — corners with foot traffic help.
