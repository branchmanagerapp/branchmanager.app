📖 Using Branch Manager — the short version

BoardThe homepage IS the to-do list.Open the app → Today board → work top to bottom. Tap a row, it opens. If a row is wrong, the RECORD is wrong — fix it or tell Claude.
QuotesGreen Send is the only send.Never copy a link from a draft — Send makes the link live and stamps it. Tax is ON at 8.375% by default; flip off per-quote if exempt.
Done?Job finished → mark completed → convert to invoice, same day.A done job without an invoice is invisible money. The board's Collect bucket chases the rest.

🤖 The AI already inside the app

📷Photo → line item.In a quote: 📷 snaps a tree and AI fills the species + line item. 📸+ bulk-loads photos — tap a photo, tap its tree.
🤖"Run AI" on any line item.Fills species, DBH, condition, suggested rate. 🌿 "2nd" gets a PlantNet second opinion. DBH hint suggests price (DBH × $100) — tap "use."
🎙️"From walkthrough" — the big one.Walk the property recording video → AI extracts the trees, hazards, urgency and builds the whole quote. Fastest way to quote a multi-tree job.
⚠️AI fills, you verify.Species + prices are suggestions. Check them before Send — your name's on it, not the robot's.
