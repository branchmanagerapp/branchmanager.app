How it works

Post what you need, someone fills it, you keep your customer.

Post the job — or the gap.Got a removal Tuesday but need a bucket truck and a climber? Post it. Slow week? Grab someone else's overflow.
A member fills it.Crew, iron, or the whole job — at set rates. Everyone's insured and on-site, so you're never up a tree alone.
You keep your customer.The job runs through your own BranchManager workspace. The client stays yours — no poaching, ever.
The point: every posted job builds one shared schedule — so we're all booked farther out, on the best work, instead of scrambling week to week. And everyone wears both hats: you're the salesman (keep your 10% on the work you sell) and the crew (get your crew rate for the work). Sell it, work it — get paid for both. And the farther out we're booked, the choosier we get — we hold our prices up, trade up to higher-quality work, and weed out the low-value jobs over time.
