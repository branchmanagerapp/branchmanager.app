How your equity works

You're a real owner — here's exactly what that means for your money. No fine print.

You build a capital account.Your buy-in, plus your share of the profit the co-op keeps each year. That's your stake in everything we own together — the trucks, the loader, the chipper.
It stays locked in while you're a member.You can't pull it out — and neither can anyone else. That's the whole point: it's what lets a handful of solo guys own real equipment and a winter reserve none of us could swing alone. Your money builds the iron instead of getting drained out.
You get paid two ways — the whole time.A fair day-rate for the work you do, that's your living. Plus cash patronage at year-end: the co-op pays out part of the profit in cash, split by what you contributed — and retains the rest into your locked capital account. So your income is wage + cash patronage, and the equity keeps building underneath it. The locked stake is the long game, never instead of a paycheck.
You're bought out when you leave.Retire or move on and your account is paid back on a set formula, over a few years so it never sinks the group. You built it — you get it. Nobody loses what they put in, and nobody walks off with the equipment fund.

The exact buy-in, the payout formula, and the tax handling get finalized with a co-op attorney + CPA — but that's the deal.

Straight answers

Do I lose my own company?

No. Your brand, your customers, your call — all yours. The co-op is the muscle behind you, not over you.

Do you control my prices?

There's a floor so nobody lowballs the group into the ground. Above that, you price your own work.

Is there a buy-in — and do I ever get it back?

Yes, and yes. The buy-in is real ownership, not a fee. It's locked in while you're a member (that's what funds our shared equipment) and bought out when you leave — see "How your equity works" above.

What if someone won't pull their weight?

Built in: you owe your share or it comes out of your equity — run it dry and you're out. It protects the people doing the work.
