How it works

Post what you need, someone fills it, you keep your customer.

Post the job — or the gap.Got a removal Tuesday but need a bucket truck and a climber? Post it. Slow week? Grab someone else's overflow.
A member fills it.Crew, iron, or the whole job — at set rates. Everyone's insured and on-site, so you're never up a tree alone.
You keep your customer.The job runs through your own BranchManager workspace. The client stays yours — no poaching, ever.
The point: every posted job builds one shared schedule — so we're all booked farther out, on the best work, instead of scrambling week to week. And everyone wears both hats: you're the salesman (keep your 10% on the work you sell) and the crew (get your crew rate for the work). Sell it, work it — get paid for both. And the farther out we're booked, the choosier we get — we hold our prices up, trade up to higher-quality work, and weed out the low-value jobs over time.
We share the good jobs — and the ugly ones. If everyone cherry-picks the gravy work, the small, far, and messy jobs never get done. So the hub balances it: everyone takes their fair share of the less-glamorous work — small cleanups, long drives, storm grunt — not just the big removals. Turn down reasonable assigned work and it shows in your standing and your patronage.

Straight answers

Do I lose my own company?

No. Your brand, your customers, your call — all yours. The network is the muscle behind you, not over you.

What's it cost to join?

Nothing — no buy-in, no fees, no merging. You bring your own company and gear. You just pay each other fair rates when you sub crew or hire iron.

Do you control my prices?

On your own jobs, you price your own work. On shared jobs there's a floor so nobody lowballs the group into the ground.

What if someone won't pull their weight?

You take your fair share of the work, good and ugly. Flake on it and the shared work — and the trust — dries up. Simple.
