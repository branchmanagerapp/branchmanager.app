🚛 Septic Pumping — The Winter Route

SRC = sourced fact · EST = market estimate, verify before betting on it. Built Aug 2026.

The thesis

Winter is the tree business's empty quarter (~$7.5k/mo nut with thin revenue). Septic pumping is scheduled, recurring, and mostly weather-proof. Northern Westchester and Putnam are heavily septic, tanks need pumping every 2–3 years, and Westchester County reimburses homeowners for pump-outs in county sewer-district areas SRC — subsidized demand. The play: book winter route days at "stay-busy" pricing (still strongly profitable — see per-job math), keep the crew and trucks earning December–March, and hold summer for tree work at full rates.

Honest winter caveats: buried lids are hard to dig in frozen ground — the model favors riser-equipped tanks, fall pre-booking (pump-before-the-holidays), and winter emergency response at premium rates (backups peak with holiday guests). Discounted scheduled work fills Nov–Dec and March; emergencies carry Jan–Feb.

Route math EST: a dense day = 4–6 stops → $600–1,300 net/day. Two route days a week through winter ≈ $5–10k net/month — roughly the winter nut, covered. The single biggest lever is the tip fee: locking a close, cheap disposal point moves every job's net by $50–100.

The three gates (all confirmable this week by phone)

1 · NYS DEC Part 364 Waste Transporter PermitRequired to haul septage. Fee for septage-only: $100 first vehicle, $200 each additional SRC. Application names your disposal facility. DEC waste transporter page · application PDF.
2 · County licensesWestchester licenses septic collectors/contractors (their homeowner reimbursements only pay county-licensed operators SRC) — Westchester DEP (914) 813-5425. Putnam Health Dept equivalent — one call each.
3 · Disposal point + fee — THE decision-makerWhich plants near Peekskill accept septage, and at what ¢/gallon, decides the whole per-job net. The NYC DEP watershed septage program tracks East-of-Hudson hauling SRC. One call to Westchester DEP answers facilities + fees; regional range 6–15¢/gal.

Also check: CDL class/endorsement for the truck GVWR chosen (tanker endorsement N for big tanks), and confirm the GL/auto policy extends — Maria call.
