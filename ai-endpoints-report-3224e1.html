One gotcha worth passing back to you

Cloudflare's free plan ships with an AI-bot block turned ON — it was returning 403 to every AI crawler at the edge, silently. All the content in the world doesn't matter if the CDN bounces the bots before they see it. If your site sits behind Cloudflare, check Security → Bots → Block AI training bots and make sure it's off. Worth 60 seconds to verify on yours.
