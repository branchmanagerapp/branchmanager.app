Second Nature Tree Service ✕ BrinkStar

Working Together — The Options

One team where each of us does what we're best at. Merit-based, written down, and accountable — so trust never has to carry what a ledger can.

What's true in every option

  • Health first. Andrew's only job right now is recovery. Everything below runs without needing him on a job site.
  • Clients stay cared for. Every BrinkStar client gets serviced under the relationship Andrew and Michelle built — nothing falls apart, nobody gets poached.
  • Every dollar is documented. Referred jobs, equipment days, and hours are tracked in Branch Manager. A monthly statement shows exactly what was earned and why.
  • Merit decides everything. Contribution is recorded, not remembered. Whatever structure we grow into, the record is the basis.
  • Nobody inherits old obligations. Each company keeps its own past. We combine forward, not backward.

The three options — lightest to fullest

Option A · Start now

Alliance — referrals, gear & people

Second Nature runs the work as the operating company. BrinkStar contributes what it has while closed, and gets paid for all of it:

  • Clients: jobs Michelle or Andrew forward are tagged "Brink-referred" in Branch Manager and pay BrinkStar a referral fee of ___% of the job, settled monthly with a statement.
  • Equipment: mini skid, trailer, chipper earn a day-rate of $___/day whenever Second Nature runs them.
  • Crew: BrinkStar's good people get hired onto Second Nature payroll (real W-2, workers' comp covered) so nobody loses a season.
  • Doors: Andrew makes the introductions he knows — prime contractors, storm networks — from wherever he is.

Paperwork: one page. Risk: near zero for both sides. Can start this week.

Option B · As Andrew recovers

Combined operation — one team, two brands

Everything in Option A, plus:

  • Roles by strength: Andrew leads business development — primes, utility/storm contracts, the bigger work. Doug leads production, bidding, and the systems. Michelle leads sales pipeline. Catherine runs operations.
  • Andrew's compensation: a per-diem or base for the development role plus a documented share of contracts he lands — written before the first one closes.
  • The contribution ledger: Branch Manager tracks who sourced, who sold, who worked, and whose gear ran on every job. Monthly statements to each person. That ledger becomes the permanent record of who built what.
  • Storm & line-clearance pursuits run under the combined banner — Second Nature's insurance and payroll, Andrew's relationships, shared upside on every contract per the written split.

Paperwork: a short working agreement. This is the engine room — most teams never need more than this.

Option C · Earned, not assumed

One company — equity from the ledger

If 6–12 months of the ledger shows a durable, balanced partnership, we form a new clean company and both brands live under it.

  • Equity is set by the contribution record — not by a handshake, not by history, not by who needed it more at the time.
  • Preconditions: BrinkStar's affairs wound down clean, roles proven in practice, both sides still wanting it.
  • If we never get here, that's fine. Option B pays everyone fairly forever. C exists for when the record makes it obvious.

This is the destination if the road proves it. The road starts at A.
