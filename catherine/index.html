Catherine doesn't need a "Catherine AI."
She needs Branch Manager working.

The honest answer after thinking it through: a separate assistant app would just be a second thing competing with the first. Her tool already exists — it's BM.
The call

Don't build a separate Catherine app

Everything a "Catherine AI" would do, Catherine already does inside Branch Manager — or should, once BM is solid. Building a parallel assistant means two places for her to look, two things to keep in sync, and a second app to maintain. That's the opposite of taking load off her.

The win isn't a new tool. It's BM being reliable and doing these jobs well.

Where the "assistant" ideas actually live

Map it to BM — most of it already exists

The features I'd sketched for a standalone app, and where they already sit in Branch Manager:
The "assistant" ideaIn Branch Manager
Today's prioritized suggestions✓ exists — the Today board on the dashboard buckets her named to-dos (send-these, write-estimate, waiting, approved→schedule, changes, invoices).
Drafted sends she approves✓ exists — BM's draft queue; nothing fires to a client without approval.
Done job → invoice ready✓ exists — jobs flow to invoicing in BM.
Schedule / run order✓ exists — the run schedule / today sheet.
Her AM/PM equipment routines◐ gap — not in BM today; a small optional add if she wants it (see below).
Voice-to-quote in the field◐ gap — a real BM feature idea, not a separate app.

Verify the exact Today-board state live in the app before relying on it — this is from build notes, not a fresh check.

What actually helps Catherine

The real to-do isn't building — it's these

  • Make BM dead reliable — the stale-cache bug that silently drops saves, and load speed, hurt her more than any missing feature. A tool she can't trust to save is worse than no tool.
  • Make sure the Today board reflects reality — it's already her front door; keep it showing what's actually to be sent/done, not stale flags.
  • Two small BM additions, only if she asks — her equipment routine as a simple checklist, and voice-to-quote. Both belong inside BM, not in a separate app.
Bottom line: scrap "Catherine AI" as a product. Her assistant is a well-running Branch Manager. That's a reliability job, not a new build.
