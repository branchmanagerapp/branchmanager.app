How it would work

One yard, one name, one set of prices.

  • One place to drop wood. Instead of logs piling up in seven driveways, everything goes to the yard.
  • Seasoned stock all year. With enough volume in one place we can season wood properly and sell dry wood, which gets a better price than green.
  • One brand, one phone number. The yard sells under its own name and sets its own prices. Customers get a real firewood business with steady supply and delivery, not a pile in someone's driveway.
  • Shared gear and labor. Processor, splitter, loader and delivery truck get used by everyone, not bought seven times.
  • Split the rent, share the income. Everyone chips in on the $3,500, and the yard pays out based on what each person puts in.

Why it's better than going it alone

  • A real yard with seasoned wood and delivery earns a better price than scattered sellers can get.
  • Your rent share is less than most of us lose in a season from dumping wood or selling it cheap.
  • Logs off your jobs turn into money instead of a disposal cost.
  • Winter income for everyone when tree work slows down.
