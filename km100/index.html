Quick troubleshooting

Won't start — starter doesn't turn

Starter motor or flat battery. Call Miniloaders.

If the machine tipped over: do NOT restart. Oil on top of the pistons can wreck the engine. See troubleshooting guide p.22.

Won't start — starter turns

1. Out of fuel? 2. Hold the key in the glow-plug (GL) position longer. 3. Put every aux hydraulic lever in neutral.

If you ran it out of fuel, bleed at the fuel filter then the injection pump (guide p.6).

No power

Clean the cyclone pre-filter → replace air filter → replace fuel filter → check no aux hydraulic function is switched on. Wrong fuel? Stop and call.

Machine won't drive

Check hydraulic fluid at the sight glass and top up. Check the park brake is fully off.

Oil pressure light on

Stop the engine and check the dipstick. If the level is fine, have the pressure checked with a gauge; if pressure is OK the switch is bad — Kubota 15841-39010.

Can't connect an attachment's hoses

Engine off, then work the hydraulic levers back and forth a few times to bleed pressure. If the attachment side is pressurized, crack its hose fitting slowly — fluid will spray.

Hydraulic fluid looks cloudy

Usually air from a hydraulic filter that wasn't pre-filled. Run it a few days and recheck. If still cloudy after oil changes, call Miniloaders.

Machine fell over

Keep the seatbelt on, stay on the machine, turn the key OFF. Don't restart it — call Miniloaders.
