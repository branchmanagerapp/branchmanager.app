2 · What "legal payroll with deductions" means for us in NY

Framed for Kevin to confirm — this is the standard checklist, not advice:

  • Pay frequency — manual workers weekly (NY DOL §191 FAQ). Semi-monthly only with a NYSDOL authorization (generally for 1,000+ employee firms). So: weekly runs, automated.
  • Federal — withholding + FICA each run; deposits monthly/semi-weekly; Form 941 quarterly (or 944 if IRS says so); 940 FUTA annual; W-2/W-3 in January. W-4 on file per employee.
  • New York — register as employer (NYS-100) if not already; NYS-45 quarterly (combined withholding + unemployment insurance wage reporting); NY SUI account; IT-2104 on file; wage-theft-prevention pay notice at hire + detailed weekly pay stubs; new-hire reporting within 20 days.
  • DBL + Paid Family Leave insurance — required NY policies, separate from workers comp. GOOD NEWS a ShelterPoint DBL policy (D701106) is on file in BM. ASK KEVIN confirm the PFL rider is attached and the policy is current/under the LLC.
  • MCTMT — Westchester is in the MCTD; small payrolls under the quarterly threshold owe none, but Kevin should confirm we're under it.
  • I-9s — federal, every employee, kept on file (digital upload into BM's compliance docs — the infrastructure already exists and is currently empty).
  • Subcontractors (Braxton-style) — W-9 on file, 1099-NEC each January, and the relationship must actually look like a sub (own tools/insurance/control). NY's construction Fair Play Act presumes employment — whether it reaches tree work is a Kevin question.
