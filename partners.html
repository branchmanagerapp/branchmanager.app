A simple way for local tree crews to swap work, share resources, and stop leaving money on the table — without merging companies or losing your name.

Why do this

Separately, we all fight the same problems: leads come in waves, jobs get underpriced, nobody has every machine, and the office work eats nights and weekends.

Together we cover more ground — big removals, pruning, pressure washing, landscaping, specialty work. One SavaTree branch runs ~20 people; between a few of us, we're already that size. The difference is they share leads, iron, and an office. We can too, while every company stays independent and keeps its own customers.

How it works — no paperwork, start today

Swap rate sheetsYou know what I charge, I know what you charge. Quoting each other's work gets easy and nobody feels shorted.
Send each other workA job that fits your crew better goes to you, and the other way around. The customer stays happy and stays local.
Run it through Branch ManagerQuotes, invoices, scheduling, and card/online payments — from your phone, so the office work stops piling up.

What Branch Manager is

It's the software Second Nature runs the whole business on — built for tree work, not adapted to it. Your account is your own: your name, your logo, your customers. Nobody else can see your numbers.

  • Quotes your customer approves from a text or email link
  • Invoices with online card payment built in
  • Schedule and jobs on your phone, made for the field
  • Customer list, job photos, and payment history in one place
