🧾 Filing Payroll Taxes Quarterly

A plain-English checklist for Second Nature Tree — what to file, when, and where the Branch Manager numbers go.

⚠️ This is a guide, not tax advice. Payroll tax has real penalties for late or wrong filings. Use this to understand the pieces and stay on schedule — but run the actual numbers and filings with your CPA, Kevin Fay (kfaycpa@gmail.com). Rates and wage bases change every year; always confirm on the IRS / NY sites below.

The two things due every quarter

If you pay W-2 employees, two returns come due four times a year:

1. Federal — Form 941

Employer's Quarterly Federal Tax Return. Reports the wages you paid, the federal income tax you withheld, and Social Security + Medicare (FICA — employer and employee halves).

2. New York — Form NYS-45

NY's combined quarterly return: state income tax withheld, wage reporting, and unemployment insurance (UI). Filed through NY Business Online Services.

Separate from these (yearly, not quarterly): W-2s to employees + Form 940 (FUTA) + 1099-NEC to subs — all due end of January. This guide is the quarterly rhythm only.

The step-by-step

1
Pull the numbers from Branch Manager. On the Payroll page, pick the pay periods in the quarter and use 📥 Export CSV. That gives you each employee's hours, gross pay, and totals — the raw figures the forms are built from.
2
Deposit taxes along the way — don't wait for the quarter to end. Federal withholding + FICA is deposited on a schedule (usually monthly for a small shop) through EFTPS. NY withholding is paid via Form NYS-1 when it accumulates. The quarterly returns just reconcile what you already deposited.
3
File Form 941 (federal). E-file through the IRS or your payroll provider; pay any balance via EFTPS. Form + instructions: irs.gov — About Form 941.
4
File Form NYS-45 (New York). File through your NY Business Online Services account (withholding + UI in one). Details: NYS-45 instructions.
5
Save the confirmations. Keep the filed 941, NYS-45, and deposit receipts each quarter — you'll need them at year-end, for the CPA, and if NYSIF or the IRS ever asks (they did for the WC audit).

The easy path

  • Hand it to Kevin (CPA). Export the quarter's CSV from Payroll, send it to Kevin Fay, and he prepares/files 941 + NYS-45. Cleanest option — you just hit the deadlines above and keep him fed the numbers.
  • Or a payroll service files it for you. If wages run through a payroll provider (e.g. Square Payroll / Gusto), it deposits and files 941 + NYS-45 automatically — your job is just approving hours in Branch Manager each week.

Branch Manager tracks hours and produces the numbers; it does not file taxes for you. Filing goes through the IRS/NY (or your CPA/payroll service).
