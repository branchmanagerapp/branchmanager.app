Emergency & Storm Work Pipeline

Decided Aug 15 2026: emergency work is the focus — it pays more, and the gear + paper are already in place. Here's the pipeline, cheapest lever first.

Why this works — the assets you already hold

  • Bucket truck fully papered: annual OSHA/ANSI inspection + dielectric boom testing completed 8/12/26 (Northeast Utility Equipment, inv #4360). Dielectric is the storm-near-wires credential most small outfits skip.
  • Insurance-restoration channel already open: the BrinkStar COI (Jul 23) lists ServPro Global as additional insured — storm/insurance work is already flowing through Brink.
  • Track record: July 5 2026 storm — 4 properties, photo-documented in BM day recaps. Plus the ASG Verizon ROW line-clearance history (fact-checked pitch on the line-clearance research page).

The moves, in order

1
Register on BidNet / Empire State Purchasing Group ~30 min, one time
One registration = bid alerts from dozens of NY local governments including City of Peekskill (they publish exclusively there). Set alerts for tree, storm, debris categories. Free basic tier; the paid tier adds broader regional alerts — start free.
bidnetdirect.com/new-york → Vendor Registration. Use info@peekskilltree.com. W9 is ready (Docs for Brink folder).
2
Get on the town lists the old-fashioned way 4 phone calls
Small towns keep informal on-call/emergency vendor lists at the DPW/Highway level — a call + COI + W9 usually does it. Maria at Tooher-Ferraris already issues per-town COIs (Peekskill, Putnam, Westchester ones exist in TREE FOLDER).
  • Cortlandt — DES Highway Division 914-977-8676 (their published storm/downed-tree line)
  • Yorktown — Town Clerk 914-962-5722 x209 (runs the bid list; they've bid "Tree Stumps and Log Removal" and Highway Dept tree removal before)
  • Peekskill — City Clerk 914-737-3400 + BidNet (step 1 covers their postings)
  • Putnam Valley — Town Hall / Highway Dept (call to ask how they hire storm tree work — not published online)
Ask each: "How do I get on your emergency/storm tree removal vendor list?" — then send COI + W9 same day.
3
Push the Brink → ServPro channel one conversation
The insurance-restoration channel pays insurer money, not homeowner money. Ask Brink directly: what would it take to be first-call for ServPro tree/storm work in northern Westchester + Putnam? (The co-op research link is already in the drafted email to him.)
4
EHAP through TCIA days, member pricing
TCIA member ID 222530 = discounted. Electrical-hazard qualification is mandatory for utility subbing (Clearway/Lewis) and is what lets a crew work storm trees tangled in lines instead of walking away from the best-paying calls. Cheapest credential-per-dollar on the board.
tcia.org EHAP program
5
Emergency page + ads on peekskilltree.com build — say go
24/7 emergency landing page (no rates shown, per standing rule) + Google presence for "emergency tree removal" searches. Queued as a build whenever you want it.
6
Utility storm rosters via Clearway after EHAP
The long game from the line-clearance research: sub under Clearway (regional, MWBE, TCIA-accredited) for utility storm surges. Needs EHAP + Z133 qualification first — step 4 feeds this.
