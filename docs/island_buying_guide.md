### Live stakes — **300x Streamco Willow** (separate sourcing)

SiteOne doesn't carry live stakes. Options ranked:

1. **HARVEST ON SITE** (cheapest) — Cut willow cuttings from a local riverbank/stream during dormancy. 18"–24" Salix purpurea or alba cuttings. Free + project-local.
2. **Cold Stream Farm** (Michigan, mail-order) — $3.01/stake at 100-499 qty = **$903 for 300**. They ship dormant Nov–Mar.
3. **Mid Atlantic Natives** — Salix sericea (silky willow) 12"/24"/36" wholesale, 100 min order, ships from VA/MD.
4. **Foggy Mountain Nursery** (NC) — willow + silky dogwood + buttonbush live stakes.

> ☎ **Call SiteOne first**: ask if they can sub-order live stakes through one of their native plant vendors. Sometimes they will.
