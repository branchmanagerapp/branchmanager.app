The two layers

1

Your private workspace

Run your business exactly like it's yours — because it is. Clients, quotes, scheduling, invoices, payments, and books, all walled off and visible to you alone. This is what you try free, today.

2

The co-op hub

A shared workspace the group owns. The jobs you choose to share roll up here so the crew never double-books, equipment gets used not idled, and the pooled expenses + profit-sharing are tracked across everyone. The hub only ever sees what members send it.
