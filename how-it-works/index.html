📅 The hidden lever: working days per year

Here's what sinks most tree businesses — your costs are annual, but you don't work 365 days. Weather, winter, breakdowns, paperwork, travel, chasing work — a tree crew is lucky to bill 150–200 days a year. Every day you don't work, the same overhead has to come out of the days you do. Fewer working days = your rate has to climb, hard.

Why this is the co-op's superpower: sharing work fills your slow days (more billable days → your required rate drops), and pooling overhead means each member carries a smaller slice of insurance, equipment & back-office. Both push that number down — same work, more left over.
