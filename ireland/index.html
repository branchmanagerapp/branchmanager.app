Ideas to share, nothing more

We're not hired and we're not pitching anything. Their in-house person built a genuinely respectable site, and their membership runs on JustGo — a proper platform we wouldn't touch. So none of this is "you should replace X."

It's just a friendly outside read: a few things — mostly quick, cheap wins — that could make what they already have work a little harder. Share the ones that seem useful, ignore the rest.

Credit where it's due

What's already good

  • Governance transparency is excellent — public strategic plan, current insurance cert, 14 years of AGM minutes. Most governing bodies hide this; they don't.
  • It's genuinely maintained — a real burst of Jan 2026 posts, current docs, active league recruitment.
  • Clear, logical structure — governance / domestic / high-performance split makes sense for an NGB.
  • Real, named humans on Contact — emails for actual people, not a black-hole form.
  • The technical foundation is sound — domain, HTTPS, redirects, sitemap, robots all correct. It's a solid base that's just showing its age.
  • They already have the hard part — the diaspora story and international results are compelling. It's credibility that's under-showcased, not missing.

If they only touched five things

The quick wins

Cheap, low-risk, high-return — most are minutes to hours, no redesign needed.
Minutes → an hour
  • Add OG / meta tags so shared links stop previewing blank
  • Fix the "@2015" footer → dynamic year
  • Re-enable pinch-zoom — delete two attributes
  • Point membership at justgo.com not the old azolve link
  • Surface the newsletter signup — the plugin's already there
More involved

Bigger ideas

A day or two each
  • A "Play / Join" homepage CTA funneling newcomers to clubs + JustGo
  • A Donate / Support button — they're literally hiring a fundraiser
  • Turn Domestic into a real club finder — map + filterable cards
Bigger projects
  • A proper Heritage Cup / diaspora hub cross-linked with heritagecuplax.com
  • Structured rosters & results instead of narrative + static archives
  • A visual + performance refresh off the 2015 theme
If we say nothing else, say these

The three that matter most

1. Their shared links preview blank.
No Open Graph tags means every time a fixture, result, or news post gets shared to WhatsApp / Facebook / X, it shows up with no image and no text. For a sport that grows on social, that's the highest-value, lowest-effort fix on the whole list.
2. They're hiring a fundraiser but have no Donate button.
There's an active "Sponsorship & Fundraising Manager" post, yet nowhere on the site to actually give money. A simple donate button meets the goodwill they're already spending money to court.
3. Their best story is orphaned.
The Heritage Cup / Team Éire diaspora narrative is their standout differentiator — and it's scattered across news posts and a second, disconnected website. Giving it one real home would make the most of what makes Irish lacrosse unique.
