Read this first · the strategic reality

They already have a member portal. Don't pitch replacing it.

Ireland Lacrosse runs JustGo (irelandlacrosse.justgo.com) — a mature, NGB-grade membership platform used by 100+ national sport bodies. Registration, dues, auto-renewals, safeguarding, coach credentials, event ticketing: all already handled. A "we'll build you a player portal from scratch" pitch walks straight into an entrenched incumbent and loses.

The winnable opening is different and cleaner:

  • Their public website is dated (old WordPress) and disconnected from the portal — this is the strong, clean deliverable.
  • A lacrosse-specific layer JustGo does poorly — heritage/eligibility verification, tryout & roster workflows, diaspora engagement.
  • Shop + donations — jerseys (they flagged this themselves) and US-diaspora fundraising, which JustGo doesn't surface on a public site.

Budget reality: volunteer-led, no paid staff (per their 2022 strategic plan), turnover fell ~€200k → ~€24k in the COVID year. This is likely a favor / portfolio / relationship play through Catherine's coaching connection — not a big-budget contract. Scope it lean and let the website be the whole Phase 1.

The organization · verified facts

Who Ireland Lacrosse is

  • National governing body for lacrosse in Ireland — men's, women's, box & Sixes under one body. Company Limited by Guarantee, est. 2009; recognized by World Lacrosse & the European Lacrosse Federation; joined the Olympic Federation of Ireland in 2025.
    ⚠️ One thing to confirm: whether they've since secured full Sport Ireland NGB status (a stated 2022 goal). Affects their funding/credibility.
  • Decision-maker: Michael "Mike" Kennedy — Founder & CEO, the public face and the person a pitch has to reach. Chair: Rudi Wortmann.
    Contact addresses are general: info@ / clubs@ / schools@irelandlacrosse.ie. No direct line published.
  • On the field: ranked ~12th (men) / 13th (women) in the world in field lacrosse. Men's U20 won European gold in 2024; women competed at the 2025 World Games. Next big date: Women's World Championship, Tokyo 2026.
  • The story worth telling: in 2020 Ireland voluntarily gave up its 2022 World Games spot to the Haudenosaunee (the originators of lacrosse, excluded on a technicality). Widely praised, 50k+ petition signatures. A genuine, verifiable brand-values asset — great website material.
  • Their defining feature — the diaspora: Ireland fields many North-American players of Irish descent via the Heritage Cup and Team Éire. Full national-team play needs passport eligibility; heritage/Éire teams accept looser Irish-ancestry criteria. This eligibility split is a real, lacrosse-specific workflow — and a US 501(c)(3) exists for diaspora fundraising.

Scope · Phase 1

The public website

This is the whole first deliverable and the one "clean prompt" target. Pages:
  • Home — hero, latest results/news, the diaspora hook, clear "Play / Support / Shop" paths
  • National Teams — men / women / box / sixes; rosters, fixtures, results, staff
  • Domestic — leagues (ILL / IWLL), club finder, "how to start playing"
  • Heritage & Diaspora — the story, Heritage Cup, Team Éire, "am I eligible?", how to get involved from abroad
  • News — editable posts; migrate the good WordPress content
  • Events & Fixtures — calendar, camps/clinics, tournaments
  • Shop — jerseys & kit (Phase 2 if it slows Phase 1)
  • Donate / Support — diaspora fundraising, one-off + recurring
  • About / Governance — board, strategic plan, partners
  • Join / Contact — routes to the JustGo portal + info@ / clubs@ / schools@

Must-haves

  • Editable by a non-coder (simple CMS or clean structured content) — so Catherine isn't blocked on us for every update
  • Mobile-first, fast, good SEO — it's the front door for players, parents & sponsors
  • Social embeds, results/fixtures, clean brand (green/orange/☘️)
  • Deep links out to the JustGo portal wherever "register / pay / renew" appears
Scope · Phase 3 (only if wanted)

The lacrosse-specific member layer

Not a JustGo replacement — the workflows it does badly. Build only what they actually ask for.

Candidate features

  • Heritage / eligibility verification — upload passport vs ancestry docs, staff review, status flag. The real differentiator.
  • Tryout / identification-event applications — apply for national-team / Éire selection online
  • Roster & team management — coach view of their squad, availability
  • Team communications — announcements to a squad/club
  • Event / camp / clinic signups — public registration + payment

Explicitly NOT rebuilding

  • Membership registration, dues, auto-renewals — JustGo owns this
  • Safeguarding & coach-credential compliance — JustGo owns this
  • Member database of record — link/integrate, don't duplicate
Open integration question: does our layer link to JustGo (simple, safe) or sync with it (harder, needs their JustGo admin + API access)? Default to link-out unless they specifically fund integration.

Open questions — this is the real work right now

Every one of these, unsettled, is a week of rework later. Answer them, and Phase 1 becomes a single clean prompt.
1. Who exactly is the client?
All of Ireland Lacrosse (the NGB), or a slice Catherine touches — a specific club, a team she coaches, or the Heritage/diaspora program? Scope and the "yes" person change completely.
2. What is this — paid, favor, or portfolio?
Sets budget, how much we build, and whether we host it or hand it off. Given their finances, assume lean.
3. Who's the decision-maker we're actually reaching?
Likely CEO Mike Kennedy. Does Catherine have a warm line to him, or are we going in cold?
4. Replace the site, or facelift it?
New standalone site vs. modernizing the existing WordPress. Affects the domain question below.
5. Domain
Take over main.irelandlacrosse.ie, sit on a subdomain, or a fresh URL for a pilot? They control the DNS — we'd need access or a handoff.
6. Do we touch JustGo at all?
Link-out only (default) or real integration (needs their JustGo admin + API). Don't promise integration blind.
7. Payments — whose Stripe for shop & donations?
Theirs (they own the money, cleaner) or ours pass-through (faster to launch, messier). US 501(c)(3) matters for tax-deductible diaspora donations.
8. Who maintains content after launch?
Catherine, a volunteer, or us on retainer? Drives how much CMS we build vs. hard-code.
9. What feeds results / fixtures / rosters?
Manual entry (simplest) vs. importing from World Lacrosse / their records. Decide before we design the data model.
10. Is there a deadline driving this?
e.g. Tokyo 2026 women's worlds, a Heritage Cup cycle, a season start. A real date sharpens Phase 1 scope.
