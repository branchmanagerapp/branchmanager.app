01 — Credit where it's due

What your site already does well

This isn't a teardown. The foundation your team built is genuinely strong — and worth keeping.

✓Governance transparency
Public strategic plan, current insurance cert, 14 years of AGM minutes. Most governing bodies hide this. You don't — it builds real trust.
✓It's genuinely maintained
A real burst of 2026 posts, current documents, active league recruitment. Someone cares about it.
✓Clear, logical structure
Governance / domestic / high-performance is exactly the right split for a national governing body.
✓Real, named humans
Actual people and emails on your Contact page, not a black-hole form. That's rare and good.
✓Sound technical base
Domain, HTTPS, redirects, sitemap — all correctly configured. It's a solid base that's simply showing its age.
✓You have the hard part
The diaspora story and international results are compelling. This is credibility that's under-showcased, not missing.
02 — The honest part

Where it's quietly holding you back

Nothing here is broken. These are the gaps between a good 2015-era site and what the game needs in 2026.

!Your shared links preview blank
No social/Open Graph tags — every fixture, result and news post shared to WhatsApp, Facebook or X shows up with no image and no text. For a sport that grows on social, that's the costliest miss on the site.
!Nowhere to give you money
You're hiring a Sponsorship & Fundraising Manager — yet there's no donate button, no shop, no sponsor page anywhere on the site.
!Your best story is orphaned
The Heritage Cup / Team Éire diaspora story lives scattered across news posts and a second, disconnected website. Your biggest differentiator has no home.
!Newcomers hit a dead end
"Domestic" is a flat text list with one catch-all email — no club finder, no map, no clear "start playing" path.
!Small signals of age
The footer still reads "©2015," the membership link points at an old address, and pinch-zoom is switched off on mobile. Tiny things that quietly say "dormant."
!The results are a story, not data
Team pages are narrative history — no live rosters, fixtures or results tables fans can actually use.

This is what people fall in love with.

Your site should make a stranger feel this in five seconds — then give them a way to play, follow, or give.

04 — The part that pays for itself

How we'd help you make money

You're already hiring to raise funds. The website should be that hire's best tool — not a brochure. Six levers, in rough order of return:

🌍

Diaspora donations

Tens of millions of people claim Irish heritage — and you already run a US 501(c)(3), so American gifts are tax-deductible. A prominent recurring + one-off "Support Irish Lacrosse" is the single biggest untapped lever you have.

Illustrative: 1,000 diaspora supporters at €10/month ≈ €120k/year
👕

Jersey & kit shop

You've said you want more people wearing the jersey. A print-on-demand shop means zero inventory and zero risk — fans buy, a partner prints and ships, you keep the margin. Identity and revenue in one.

Illustrative: 400 jerseys/year at ~€25 margin ≈ €10k/year, no stock held
🤝

A real sponsorship storefront

Give your new fundraiser a page to sell: tiered sponsor packages, logo placement, sponsored match content, and clear reach numbers. Right now a sponsor has nothing to look at.

🎟️

Events, camps & the Heritage Cup

On-site paid registration and ticketing for camps, clinics and the Heritage Cup — instead of email back-and-forth. Every event becomes a clean, countable revenue line.

🎫

A sharper membership funnel

JustGo stays as your system of record — we just build a far clearer "Become a Member" path into it. Every extra percentage point of conversion is recurring dues you're currently leaving on the table.

📣

Audience that compounds

The social tags, an embedded feed and a newsletter signup turn one-off visitors into an owned audience — which is exactly what makes every sponsorship and appeal above worth more next year.

On the numbers: the figures above are illustrative — arithmetic to show the shape of each lever, not forecasts. Real projections need your actual traffic, membership and diaspora data, which we'd model together before anyone commits to anything.
