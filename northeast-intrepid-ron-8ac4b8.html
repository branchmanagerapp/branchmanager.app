From Doug Brown · Second Nature Tree · June 2026

I'm working to become the Intrepid compact-loader dealer for the Tri-State, based at the Highland Industrial Park site. Here's the honest truth: to land that dealership, I need a good mechanic locked in — someone who can set up, service, and repair the machines. You're who I'd want. So I've been kicking around an idea that I think makes a lot of sense for both of us: put Rescue Repair here, at the site.

The idea

Set up Ron's Rescue Repair as a real shop at the site — a fixed place customers come to, with your sign on it — instead of working only out of the truck. You run your business; I run the loaders; we share the space and split the rent. It makes the rent cheaper for both of us, and it keeps you busy with steady work.

Straight up — this is an early idea, and I don't have every detail figured out yet. I'm not asking you to commit to anything. But locking in a mechanic is what gives me a real shot at the Intrepid deal, and the more I look at it, the more this setup makes sense for both of us.

What's in it for you

  • A home base for the shop — a real location and address for Rescue Repair, not just pickup-and-drop-off. People can find you, drop off, and see the sign.
  • Cheaper than your own place — we split the rent, so you get a shop for a fraction of what going solo would cost.
  • Built-in customers — the tree guys, landscapers, and farmers coming through for loaders also need their mowers, saws, blowers, and generators fixed. That walk-in flow is yours.
  • Steady extra income, on your schedule — keep busy, fill the slow stretches, all under your own business.
  • Plus the loader service work — be the service partner on the Intrepids (setup, warranty, repairs) for more steady work when you want it.

How it would work

  • You keep your own business — Rescue Repair stays yours; this just gives it a home and more customers.
  • We split the rent on fair terms numbers to work out together.
  • Your hours, your call — work the shop and the loader service as much or as little as you want.
  • Nothing's locked until you've seen the space and we've talked it through.
