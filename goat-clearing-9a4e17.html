🐐 Goat Land Clearing — the 2-Goat Pilot

Every number tagged EST is a market estimate with a source — nothing here is booked revenue.

The idea

Goats eat the brush machines can't reach and chemicals shouldn't touch — steep banks, poison ivy, wetland edges, under structures. As an add-on to tree & brush quotes it's a differentiator no competitor around Peekskill offers, and it markets itself (people stop for goats, not for chippers). Two goats is not a clearing crew — it's a pilot: prove demand as a quote line-item, learn husbandry cheaply, scale to a working herd of 8–12 only if the phone rings.

Revenue potential — honest version

Market rates for goat clearing EST: $400–$1,000 per acre typical, up to $2,000–$4,000/acre for dense brush — and the Northeast runs 20–40% above national average. But per-acre pricing assumes a herd. Two goats realistically service small residential patches (⅛–¼ acre) over one–two weeks each.

What to know before buying

  • Goats are herd animals — two is the correct minimum. Wethers (neutered males) are the cheap, easy brush eaters.
  • Check Cortlandt/Putnam Valley zoning for keeping livestock at the yard/home parcel before purchase.
  • Poison ivy is goat candy — safe for them, and that's the premium residential use case.
  • Toxic for goats: rhododendron, mountain laurel, azalea, yew, wilted cherry — walk every site first; these are everywhere in Westchester landscaping.
  • Water + shade + nightly predator-safe fencing on jobs (coyotes are real here).
