The thing the math can't show

This is your work truck for the tree business you're keeping — and as a Cummins diesel it's worth roughly what you owe, so there's no underwater problem to escape. The only scenario where "let it go" even comes up is a Chapter 7 — and even there, a truck with equity is one you'd reaffirm/keep, not hand over (the trustee can reach equity above NY's ~$4,825 exemption).
