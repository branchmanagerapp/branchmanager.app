Branch Manager — what it costs to run

Itemized monthly burn to keep it stable, secure, and not silently broken. Tiered by how much it matters.

Where you are right now: mostly free tiers (verified today — Supabase org has no paid plan). No crash risk this second, but the free tier has no automated backups and email caps at 100/day. The two Tier-1 upgrades below are the ones I'd actually pay for.

Tier 1 — don't embarrass yourself with real customers

Required~$45/mo
Supabase Pro$25/mo
Free tier auto-pauses your database after 7 idle days and keeps only 1-day backups. Pro = daily backups + 7-day point-in-time recovery + no auto-pause. This is the one I'd buy first — it's your insurance against losing data.
Resend Pro$20/mo
Free tier = 100 emails/day, and we've hit it. Fine for receipts today; the day a busy morning of invoices + review requests runs, BM silently stops sending mid-day. Pro removes the cap.

Tier 2 — strongly recommended for production

Recommended~$33/mo
UptimeRobot Solo$7/mo
Texts your phone the moment the app or a payment function goes down — before your crew calls you about it. Free tier works too (5-min checks, email only).
Sentry Team$26/mo
Catches every JS error from every session (it's what caught the quote-delete crash). Free tier's 5k events/mo is fine until you have a few crew logins.

Tier 3 — only once you grow

At scale~$5/mo
Cloudflare Workers Paid$5/mo
Free tier = 100k requests/day. You're well under. Only matters at 5+ companies on the platform.
