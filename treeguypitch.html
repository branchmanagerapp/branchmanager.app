TREE GUY SUMMER CAMP™

A modest proposal from the guys who just pulled forty trees off your cabins — and accidentally invented the best vacation in America.

Dear Postcard Cabins,

In July a storm dropped a small forest on your Western Catskills camp. You called us. We came up with a bucket truck, a chipper, a mini skid steer, and three guys who genuinely cannot believe they get paid to do this.

Somewhere around day two — rigging a maple off a cabin roof while the sun came through the hemlocks — we noticed something. Your guests kept watching us. Not politely. Longingly. Grown adults who spend $300 a weekend to sit in a beautiful black box and "digitally detox" were standing at a respectful distance, watching a chipper eat a brush pile the way kids watch fireworks.

People pay $4,000 for wellness retreats where the activity is journaling. We are prepared to offer something better: actual trees, actual ropes, actual purpose.

The idea

One week a season: Tree Guy Summer Camp. Your guests — the city kind, the ones whose most dangerous tool is a stand mixer — pay to spend a week working alongside a real, insured, professional tree crew on your property. Your dangerous trees get managed. Your trails get cleared. Your firewood gets stacked into pornographically neat rows. And your guests go home with calluses, a knot they'll show everyone at Thanksgiving, and the best story in their entire friend group.

You read that right: they pay. To do our job. On your land. Tom Sawyer ran this exact play with a fence in 1876 and it's still undefeated.

A day at camp

7:00 AMDeli run. Bacon-egg-and-cheese on a roll. Non-negotiable. This is culture now.
8:00 AMMorning circle. Stretch, safety talk, everyone gets a helmet and a trail name. (You're "Chipmunk" now. It wasn't a vote.)
9:00 AMKnots & ropes school. Bowline, clove hitch, running bowline. The same rigging we used to lower a tree off your cabin without scratching the gutter.
11:00 AMClimb time. On rope, on harness, on a closed course tree, with a certified climber an arm's length away. Fifteen feet up a hemlock changes a person.
1:00 PMChipper hour. Guests feed brush into the chipper under supervision. We have watched hedge-fund guys achieve total inner peace doing this.
3:00 PMFirewood Olympics. Split, stack, judge each other. Winner gets the good camp chair.
6:00 PMFirepit. The very firepit we un-buried this July. S'mores. Sore arms. Enormous feelings.
