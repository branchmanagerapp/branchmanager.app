Catherine × Second Nature Tree LLC
The Partnership, on One Page

Plain-English summary of the Amended & Restated Operating Agreement (draft, Feb 2026). The full agreement is the legal document — this page just makes it easy to talk through.

The deal in one sentence

Catherine becomes a co-owner starting at 5% today, and can earn her way to a full 50/50 partnership by growing the company — 5% more each year the business hits its revenue target and keeps a 12% profit margin.

What Catherine gets on day one

  • 5% ownership immediately, with full voting and distribution rights.
  • No buy-in required — zero capital contribution; her stake is earned through the work.
  • It's a profits interest: she shares in everything the company earns and grows from now on (not in the pre-existing equipment/assets — that stays Doug's).

The two safety valves

Miss a revenue target?
One carry-forward: hit the next year's target and you catch up both steps at once. (Miss $400k in Yr 1, hit $600k in Yr 2 → jump 5% → 15%.)
Miss the 12% margin?
Catherine picks one: skip that year's step (can catch up later), or top up the profit gap in cash and vest anyway. No election in 60 days = skip.

Pay

Owner pay is drawn from free cash flow (what's left after expenses, debt, taxes, and reserves) and split in proportion to vested ownership. Doug, as Managing Member, calls when cash flow supports owner pay.

Catherine's side of the bargain

Beyond fieldwork, Catherine owns: the books (bookkeeping, AP/AR, payroll, reporting), insurance (GL, workers' comp, vehicle), licenses & compliance, and driving the 12% margin. The agreement is explicit: doing these well is how the stake is earned.

If it ever ends

  • Unvested percent is forfeited; everything already vested is hers to keep.
  • The company can (but doesn't have to) buy back her vested share at book value, paid over 5 years with interest (prime + 2%).
  • No selling or transferring ownership to outsiders without consent; the company gets first refusal.
This is a summary, not the contract. Where anything here differs from the signed agreement, the agreement wins. Source: “Catherine Vesting.docx” (Amended & Restated Operating Agreement of Second Nature Tree LLC, draft) — emailed to Catherine Feb 18, 2026. A lawyer should review before signing.
