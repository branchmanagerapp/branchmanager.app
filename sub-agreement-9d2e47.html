  1. 1. You are your own business. Subcontractor is an independent contractor, not an employee. You supply and pay your own people, run your own equipment, carry your own taxes, and give Second Nature a W-9 before anything rolls. Second Nature issues a 1099. No one is paid in cash, ever.
  2. 2. Paper before wheels. Before mobilization you deliver a Certificate of Insurance naming Second Nature Tree LLC as certificate holder and additional insured for: General Liability, Commercial Auto on every vehicle you bring, and Workers' Compensation (NY requires it for anyone with employees; a sole owner with no employees must show the NYS exemption). Limits are whatever the prime's state-specific requirement sheet says for that deployment; Second Nature passes that sheet down to you unchanged. No COI, no deployment.
  3. 3. Every deployment gets a Deployment Sheet. One page from Second Nature before you leave the yard: prime and site, the rates that apply (from the attached Rate Schedule or the prime's sheet), the commute allowance, the expense policy, and the desk phone number for daily reports. Nothing on this Agreement changes; the sheet fills in the blanks for that job.
  4. 4. Portal-to-portal, logged daily, or it didn't happen. Hours run from leaving your yard to returning to your yard, with the commute allowance on the Deployment Sheet (Roscoe 2026 used 2 hours, 2.5 hours for the bucket). By 9:00 pm every day you text the desk: each person's full name, time left yard, arrived on site, left site, home; equipment used; photos of the emergency work (photos, not videos). Hours that are not logged that day are not billed and cannot be added later. Only hours the prime accepts are payable.
  5. 5. Pay when paid, and fast when it lands. Second Nature invoices the prime from the accepted logs. Second Nature pays you by check or ACH within 5 business days of receiving the prime's payment for that period, at the rates on the Deployment Sheet. Advances or progress wires from the prime are passed through to you pro rata as they arrive. If the prime short-pays or does not pay, Second Nature pursues it and shares whatever is collected pro rata; Second Nature does not guarantee the prime's payment. Your invoice must match the accepted log or it waits.
  6. 6. Expenses. Fuel, meals or per diem, and lodging follow the prime's policy on the Deployment Sheet. Keep every receipt. Reimbursable items are passed through when the prime reimburses them.
  7. 7. Safety and conduct. ANSI Z133 practices, PPE on, no alcohol or drugs on a deployment, and anyone can stop work for a hazard. Property or third-party damage is reported to the desk the same day and is covered by your insurance. You are responsible for your own equipment.
  8. 8. Don't go over heads. For 12 months after any deployment you will not contact or solicit the prime, the property owner, or their insurer or adjuster directly about that work or future work reached through it. The relationship stays with whoever landed the call. If you land the call, you are the desk, this Agreement applies in reverse, and Second Nature is your subcontractor on the same terms.
  9. 9. Term. Rolling. Either side may end it by written notice; it stays in force for any deployment already under way. Not exclusive. New York law. This is the whole agreement; changes must be in writing and signed by both.
